How it works

  1. Validation. Only A, C, G and T are accepted. FASTA header lines, spaces and digits are removed. Any other character is highlighted with its position.
  2. Transcription. The coding strand is copied with T replaced by U. A template strand is reverse-complemented first.
  3. Translation. The mRNA is read three bases at a time using the standard genetic code, from the first AUG until a stop codon.
